## 📖 About PRS Gaming

**PRS Gaming** is an American esports organisation founded in **2013** in **Long Island, New York**.

The name originally stood for **Precision Gaming**. When other clans began using the same name, the organisation kept the initials **PRS**, which stand for:

> **POWER RAGE SUCCESS**

PRS is primarily known for competitive **Call of Duty**. The organisation remains active and relaunched in **2026**.

## 📜 Our Story

### 2013 — Founding in Long Island

PRS Gaming was founded in 2013 in Long Island, New York, by three real-life best friends — **Blade, DUB11N and Kamz** — who started the team out of a passion for gaming and YouTube.

It began under the name **Precision Gaming**; when other clans started using that name, the team shortened it to **PRS**, standing for **Power Rage Success**.

### 🎯 Sniping Roots — How PRS Got Known

PRS first got known as a **strictly sniper-only and quickscoping team** for Call of Duty.

That style built the team's early reputation and following, and PRS became a **well-known team on the East Coast**, with people knowing the team from **NJROD** and the tournaments PRS played in there.

### 2015 — The Night Stalker Era Begins

In 2015, **DUB11N and Kamz fell off**, and **Night Stalker** came in, taking their spot alongside Blade — the partnership (**PRS-Blade & PRS-Nightstawker**) listed as the organisation's owners on the Call of Duty Esports Wiki.

### 🏆 Tournament Era — AGN, NAL and Challengers

From sniping, PRS moved into online Call of Duty tournaments and became a winning organisation, accomplishing **many tournament victories** online across:

- **AGN**
- **NAL**
- **Call of Duty Challengers**

— over the years through to 2025, including:

> ### 🥇 3x Back-to-Back AGN Championships

In **2023**, PRS also **bought the NAL** when the league went out of business.

### 🌍 Worldwide — NA and EU Rosters

PRS grew worldwide, with players from:

### 👀 Recognition Beyond Call of Duty

The PRS name carried weight outside the game:

- UFC champion **Cris Cyborg** recognised PRS Gaming, was a fan of the team, and tweeted about / liked the team's post — the post is on PRS Gaming's social media.
- Former PRS member **PRS PRIME** was given **VIP access to a Milwaukee Brewers baseball game** because he was known from PRS.
